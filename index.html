About me

Hi, I’m Sofia! I’m an oboist, writer, and visual artist based in Maine. I’m also drawn to physics, math, and architecture.

For music, I’ve played in an array of groups across Maine, ranging from youth ensembles to the Portland Symphony Orchestra. Away from the oboe, you’ll find me improvising at the piano, sketching buildings, or out on the tennis court.
